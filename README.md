
# 🎲 Ludo-Party (Cờ Cá Ngựa)

Welcome to **Ludo-Party**, a modern twist on the classic Vietnamese board game. Play with friends or challenge opponents online in two exciting modes: **Basic** and **Funny**.

---

## 🎮 Game Modes

- **Basic Mode**  
  Play by the traditional rules of *Ludo-Party*. Roll the dice, move your pieces, and race to bring all your horses home.

- **Funny Mode**  
  Experience a chaotic and entertaining version of the game with special effect tiles:
  - **Bomb**: Explodes and sends your piece back.  
  - **Shield**: Protects your piece from being knocked out.  
  - **Move Forward**: Advance a set number of steps instantly.  
  - **Move Backward**: Retreat a set number of steps.  
  - **Kick Random Piece**: Knock out a random opponent’s piece.  
  - **Spawn New Piece**: Add an extra piece to the board.  

---

## 🚀 How to Play

1. Choose between **Basic Mode** or **Funny Mode**.  
2. Roll the dice and move your pieces strategically.  
3. In Funny Mode, land on special tiles to trigger effects.  
4. Be the first to bring all your horses home to win!
